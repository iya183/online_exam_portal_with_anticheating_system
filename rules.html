Proctoring Rules & System Audits

  • Fullscreen Requirement: The exam will run in full-screen mode. Exiting fullscreen will trigger a proctoring violation.
  • Tab/Window Monitoring: Navigating away from this tab, minimizing, or switching windows is strictly forbidden and logged.
  • Input Restraints: Right-click context menus, copy, cut, and paste shortcuts are disabled for security.
  • Webcam Feed: A live webcam preview will monitor your workspace. No videos are uploaded, this is a local simulation.
  • Termination Threshold: Accumulating 3 violations will auto-submit your exam and lock you out of retaking this exam until an administrator unlocks you.
You will enter fullscreen and activate proctoring tools upon starting.
